Privacy Policy
Effective September 23, 2026 · Last updated September 23, 2026

We don't collect your personal data.

Bad Plot is a daily movie-guessing game. This page explains, plainly, what the app stores and where. Right now, that's a very short list, and every bit of it stays on your device.

What we store

Everything lives on your device

Bad Plot doesn't require an account, doesn't have a server, and doesn't send anything about you or your play over the network. Your progress is saved locally on your phone using standard on-device storage, the same way a note-taking app remembers your notes.

Stored locallyPurpose
Today's wrong guesses & puzzle statusSo the app remembers your progress if you close it mid-puzzle
Streak, best streak, games played, win rateShown on the Stats screen
Whether you've seen the "How to Play" screenSo it only shows once
What we don't do

No accounts, no tracking, no third parties

  • No sign-up, login, email address, or name is ever requested.
  • No analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs are included in the app.
  • Nothing is uploaded to a server. There isn't one. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.
  • We don't sell, share, or have access to any data, because none leaves your device in the first place.
Children's privacy

Safe for any age, by design

Because Bad Plot collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. There's no account system that could capture a child's identity, location, or contact details.

If this changes

Future features get their own disclosure

If Bad Plot later adds something that involves a third party, for example an affiliate "watch it now" link, an ad-supported hint unlock, or an online daily-puzzle sync, this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and it will say plainly what's collected, by whom, and why.
